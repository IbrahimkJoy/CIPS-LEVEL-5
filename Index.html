Module Purpose (from syllabus)On completion of this module, learners will be able to evaluate the different schools of thought and the reasoning behind the variety of approaches to the management of themselves, individuals and groups or teams within organisations.
Module Aim(s) (from syllabus)Management is the administration of an organisation, whether it be a business, a not-for-profit organisation, or government body. Those who manage procurement and supply activity must possess expertise in developing and fulfilling organisational and functional objectives. Learners gain a wider appreciation of theories and techniques that relate to managing teams and individuals involved with the procurement and supply function.

Module Purpose (from syllabus)On completion of this module, learners will be able to appraise a variety of tools and techniques to establish the level of risk in supply chains and to recommend and apply ways of avoiding, mitigating or managing risk.
Module Aim(s) (from syllabus)Risk management is a central part of an organisation's strategy. The supply chain is by its very nature a complex activity which is therefore susceptible to risk and its associated impacts. This module enables learners to examine the nature of supply chain risk and to evaluate mitigation techniques available. Learners will undertake risk analysis, consider the advantages of a risk register, and apply a range of appropriate risk management tools and techniques to the supply chain. ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) considerations are fully integrated throughout this updated module.

Module Purpose (from syllabus)On completion of this module, learners will be able to explain the main legal aspects of contracts for procurement and supply, and select appropriate remedies to breach of contract and to manage disputes.
Module Aim(s) (from syllabus)The contracting process is a critical element of the procurement and supply role. Contracts need to be properly formed and managed to reduce the risk of dispute or contractual failure. This module is designed for those with responsibility for managing contracts and requires learners to apply legal concepts and contractual measures to minimise risk, and to evaluate responses to contractual disputes.
